# City Computing Collective Code of Conduct

The City Computing Collective is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and intellectually honest community.

## Expected behavior

- Engage ideas critically while treating people respectfully.
- Make evidence, uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest visible.
- Credit contributors, data providers, communities, and prior work appropriately.
- Respect privacy, data licenses, research agreements, and community consent.
- Use clear titles, relevant categories, and sufficient context when posting.
- Report harmful, deceptive, discriminatory, or unsafe behavior to moderators.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, impersonation, or fabricated affiliations.
- Misrepresentation of evidence, authorship, project status, or data rights.
- Sharing confidential data, credentials, or restricted materials.
- Using community participation to pressure others into unpaid or uncredited labor.

## Enforcement

Moderators may edit categories, request corrections, hide or remove content, lock discussions, limit participation, or remove accounts when necessary. Significant moderation decisions should be documented and open to appeal.

This founding policy will be revised transparently as the community grows.
