Join the community

One account. Four simple steps.

The Collective is free and publicly readable. GitHub currently provides identity, posting, replies, and notifications—so there is no separate community password.

Who can join?

Researchers, students, practitioners, developers, public institutions, community organizations, and anyone engaging seriously with computational urban questions.

Onboarding

Move from reader to contributor.

Create or sign inUse a free GitHub account. Existing GitHub users do not need another login.
Read the guidelinesReview the Code of Conduct, privacy expectations, and community norms.
Introduce your workShare your role, urban questions, geography, methods, and collaboration goals.
Follow and contributeJoin discussions, share a project, post a resource, or answer a question.
