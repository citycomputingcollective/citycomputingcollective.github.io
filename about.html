About the Collective

An open home for city computing research.

We connect computational methods with urban theory, local knowledge, public questions, and reproducible evidence.

Community before platform

The Collective is in its founding phase. Governance and infrastructure will grow transparently with actual participation.

Mission

Connect fragmented communities

Bring researchers, students, practitioners, public institutions, and open-source contributors into sustained collaboration.

Practice

Turn conversation into artifacts

Encourage documented data, code, methods, projects, papers, tools, benchmarks, and city-facing outputs.

Values

Be rigorous and responsible

Make assumptions, limitations, provenance, credit, uncertainty, and affected communities visible.

Governance

Founding principles.

Open participationAnyone acting in good faith may read, ask, share, and contribute.
Transparent moderationRules, category changes, and major community decisions should be documented.
Respect and safetyHarassment, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and deceptive promotion are not accepted.
Research integrityDo not misrepresent evidence, authorship, affiliations, data rights, or project status.
Community ownershipContributors receive visible credit; shared infrastructure should avoid dependence on one individual.
