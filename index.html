What is city computing?

Urban questions, computational methods, local knowledge.

City computing connects urban theory with spatial data and computation. It brings together urban science, GIS, sensing, simulation, data engineering, and responsible AI to study how cities work—and how urban knowledge can become more open, rigorous, and useful.

ObserveSensing, imagery, mobility and administrative data
ModelSpatial analysis, simulation, ML and urban AI
InterpretUrban theory, lived experience and local context
ShareOpen data, code, benchmarks and research outputs

Open research lifecycle

Ideas should become inspectable artifacts.

The Collective supports the path from a well-framed city question to a team, evidence, feedback, and reusable outputs.

01 / QUESTIONFrame the problem

Define the urban context, stakeholders, and evidence gap.

02 / TEAMConnect expertise

Bring together domain knowledge, methods, data, and place.

03 / BUILDWork in the open

Share milestones, assumptions, limitations, and requests for feedback.

04 / RELEASEPublish the result

Release documented code, data, papers, benchmarks, or tools.
