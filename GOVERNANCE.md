# City Computing Collective Governance

## Founding stage

The Collective currently operates as a small, open community project. Initial maintainers are responsible for infrastructure, moderation, onboarding, and documenting decisions.

## Decision levels

1. **Routine moderation and maintenance** — handled by moderators and maintainers.
2. **Category, template, and workflow changes** — proposed publicly and documented in community discussions.
3. **Mission, governance, partnerships, and major platform changes** — require a public proposal, a comment period, and a recorded decision.

## Contributor pathway

Members may progress from participant to recurring contributor, moderator, project maintainer, or steering contributor based on sustained, constructive work—not popularity alone.

## Conflicts of interest

People making decisions about funding, sponsorship, selection, review, or moderation should disclose relevant conflicts and step back when impartiality is reasonably in doubt.

## Transparency

Major community decisions, governance revisions, sponsorship relationships, and moderation-policy changes should remain publicly accessible.
