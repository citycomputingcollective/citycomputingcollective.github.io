Every resource needs context.

DatasetGeography, time, resolution, variables, license, provenance, processing, and known quality limitations.
Tool or methodUrban problem, intended users, inputs, outputs, installation, example, validation, and maintenance status.
Model or agentIntended task, training data, evaluation, failure modes, geographic limits, usage constraints, and citation.
BenchmarkResearch question, data splits, metrics, baselines, submission rules, and geographic generalization.
