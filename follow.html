How the channels work

Email

Email: Blogtrottr, a free third-party service, can email you each new item. It shows ads, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Feed reader

Feed reader: apps such as Feedly collect updates from many sites in one place. Copy the address and add it to your reader.

Calendar

Calendar: subscribe once and new events appear in your calendar by themselves. Google Calendar can take up to a day to show changes.

GitHub

GitHub: if you have a GitHub account, open the repository, choose Watch, then Custom, and tick Discussions. GitHub then notifies you of every new discussion.

Stop following

Each service has its own way to stop. This site cannot do it for you, because it does not know who follows.

  • Email: use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.
  • Feed reader: remove the feed in your reader.
  • Calendar: remove the calendar in your calendar app.
  • GitHub: open the repository, choose Watch, then choose Unwatch.

This site does not store anything about you. Email and reader services are run by the companies named here.
